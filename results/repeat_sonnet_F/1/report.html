Do design agents earn their cost?

Every proposer runs on every case. A case is solved when at least one proposal passes all seven requirements. Regret is how much heavier the chosen design is than the true optimum found by brute force. A false pass is a design the proposer claimed would pass that fails strength or stiffness. Crashes are recorded and left out of the scores.
